Cantoria (Musician)

Tool: Musical Instrument
Attribute: Agility or Charisma

  • Ceremonial Bagpipes - A blessed leather sac fitted with sanctified brass pipes that creates haunting melodies when compressed, as consecrated air escapes through the tubes. Often inscribed with Imperial aquilas and used in regimental ceremonies.
  • War Drum - A percussion instrument crafted from tanned grox hide stretched over reinforced adamantium frames, sounded by striking with blessed drumsticks or gauntleted fists. Used to coordinate troop movements and inspire battlefield courage.
  • Vox-Flute - A hollow tube of sanctified bone or blessed metal, perforated with precisely calculated holes. Played by channeling breath through the instrument while manipulating finger placements to create notes that can carry across vast battlefields.
  • Imperial Harp - A stringed instrument with a triangular adamantium frame, strung with wire blessed by Tech-Priests. Played by plucking its mono-molecular strings, often used in noble courts and aboard command vessels during ceremonial occasions.
  • Battle Horn - A curved tube forged from the horn of a great beast or crafted from sanctified brass, played by forcing air through the instrument. Capable of producing sounds that can rally troops or strike fear into the Emperor’s enemies.
  • Ecclesiarchy Lute - A stringed instrument with an elongated neck marked with holy frets, played by pressing fingers against blessed wire strings and plucking them to create devotional melodies that honor the God-Emperor.
  • Harmonium Imperialis - A massive keyboard instrument encased in a baroque adamantium frame, containing hundreds of blessed metal strings struck by sacred hammers when keys are depressed. Often found in cathedral-ships and cardinal worlds, producing music fit for the Emperor’s ears.

Attribute: Agility or Intellilgence

These devices can be appeased, modified, or restored using Omnissiah’s Implements—though such work without proper Mechanicus training risks awakening the machine-spirit’s wrath or inviting daemonic possession into the sacred mechanisms.

Vehicles

Attribute: Agility, Intelligence or Might

  • ** Naval Vessels** - Imperial Navy frigates, cruisers, battleships, and massive Emperor-class battleships. Also includes smaller craft such as Aquila landers, gun-cutters, and planetary patrol boats used for oceanic operations.
  • Void Craft - Escort vessels, strike cruisers, and the mighty battlebarges of the Space Marines. Also encompasses smaller void-capable craft such as Thunderhawks, boarding torpedoes, and Fury interceptors used for ship-to-ship operations in the cold vacuum of space.
  • Ground Vehicles - Sacred war machines such as Leman Russ battle tanks, Chimera armored transports, Imperial Guard Sentinels, and the mighty Land Raiders of the Adeptus Astartes. Also includes civilian transports like cargo-haulers and Rhino APCs.
  • Machine-Spirit Communion - Proper operation requires knowledge of activation rituals, blessed oils and incense for maintenance, and the appropriate hymns to keep the machine-spirits content during operation. Failure to observe these rites may result in mechanical failure or worse—machine-spirit rebellion.

Note: Pilot certification requires Imperial authorization and proper benediction by a Tech-Priest or certified Enginseer.
